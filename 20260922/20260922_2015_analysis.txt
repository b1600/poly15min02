What the script does

It replays every window in var/poly15m.db that has both a corrected open_price and at least one recorded order-book snapshot (2,168 windows as of last night) through the exact live strategy code — PaperTrader/PositionManager/RiskGate/PaperExecutor, unmodified — using the _RollingBars speedup from earlier. Three parts:

1. list_backtestable_markets(db_path) — pulls the eligible condition_ids from the DB.
2. run_backtest(db_path, ids, settings) — the actual replay. This is the ~40-45 minute part.
3. Per-window PnL from fills + resolved_outcome — computed directly from the ground truth (payout minus cost minus fee per window), not from PaperExecutor.realized_pnl (a cumulative counter, unreliable across restarts). Gives mean/window, sd, t-stat, and a day-by-day breakdown.

Results print to stdout and save to var/full_history_backtest_result.json.
