-----------------------INTERPRETATION 29 Sep 2026
Verdict under the pre-registered rule: NO FIX SELECTED. Step 5 fails.

  - The highest train PnL is floor2.4 (+$246 vs base), but its paired t of 1.41 is below the required 2.
  - Every variant, including base, loses money on test (Sep 23 onward). None would PASS even if
    selected: the best test result (volmin2.4, -$107) is still a loss.
  - Train rankings don't carry over to test. The two strongest train improvements reverse:
      volmin1.5  train +$234 (paired t 2.00)  ->  test -$36 vs base (paired t -0.71)
      floor1.5   train +$218 (paired t 1.83)  ->  test -$81 vs base (paired t -1.71)
    The only variant that cleared paired t >= 2 on train (volmin1.5, not selected because the rule
    picks by total PnL) would also have failed on test (-$229.78).
  - maxT480, the "no entries with more than 8 minutes left" idea from the Sep 28 notes, cut train PnL
    from +$305 to +$111. The early entries were not what lost money on train.
  - Every candidate works by trading less (fewer windows traded). On train that removes losing trades;
    on test the remaining trades still lose. Filtering an unprofitable signal doesn't create an edge.

What this means
  - Together with step 4 (backtest matches live) and the tuning split (Aug 3 - Sep 7 in-sample),
    there is still no evidence that this fair-value model has an edge outside the data it was tuned on.
    Adding filters doesn't change that.
  - Step 6 (a new forward paper run) has nothing to test: no fix passed, and base is known to lose.
  - Options, in the order I'd consider them:
      1. Fix CLOB price_change handling (the order book is currently only updated on snapshots), then
         re-run base on the full history. That is a data fix, not tuning. If the edge is still absent,
         the model itself is the problem.
      2. Rethink the signal instead of filtering it. On Sep 26-28 the market was better calibrated
         than the model. A variant that trades only where the model disagrees with a market that has
         been measured as miscalibrated needs new analysis, not a new filter.
      3. Stop this strategy and keep the recorder running (Binance + CLOB + Chainlink) for future work.
  - None of the three new settings should be turned on live. They are off by default and can stay
    in the code for future sweeps.
