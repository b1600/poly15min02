That's not a footnote — it's the mechanism behind everything above. An overconfident p_up_fair inflates net_edge, so the strategy takes trades that have no real edge and pays 2% to do it. Two caveats on that log-loss, though. Rows are logged every decision tick, so they're heavily correlated within a window (295/134 windows, not 160k/69k independent samples).
